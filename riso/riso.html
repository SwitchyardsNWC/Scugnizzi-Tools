Riso separator

Split a picture into two or three Riso inks, then print it on paper that isn't perfectly registered.

Image

Save to project puts the print beside its picture, at the press size, and keeps its settings with the project.

Photos and flat graphics both work. Scaled to 900 px for the press and kept in this browser.

Presets

A preset sets the inks and the press, not the image.

Saved presets belong to the project: every tool on this site offers them, Freeform included.

Reset clears everything this tool saved in this browser, image included.
