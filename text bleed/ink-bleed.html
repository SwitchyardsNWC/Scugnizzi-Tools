Layers

Click a layer to edit it, drag it on the paper to place it. Every .svg in the assets folder is offered as a stamp (↻ after adding files), or drop an .svg onto the paper. With a project open, the SVG pictures in its assets are stamps too, and they come back after a reload.

Editing

__double underscores__ underline. Leading spaces are kept, so you can indent by hand.
