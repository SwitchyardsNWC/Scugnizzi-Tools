Split-flap board

A departure board. Type a message; every letter clatters into place.

Messages

The highlighted message is on the board. Editing it flips the changed letters live. Cycling pauses while you type. Space flips to the next message, 1–9 jump to one.

Presets

A preset replaces the messages, board, type, motion and export settings. Reset clears everything saved for this tool.
